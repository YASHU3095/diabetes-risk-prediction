WE'RE HERE TO HELP

Help & important information

Is this a definitive medical diagnosis?

No. DiabetesCare AI is an educational major project prototype designed for risk screening and lab capacity optimization. Only a licensed physician can diagnose diabetes using venous blood laboratory tests (HbA1c or Fasting Plasma Glucose).

What is the 30% laboratory testing capacity rule?

In resource-constrained clinics, reagent testing kits and centrifuge slots are limited. Rather than turning away patients randomly, the system ranks all arrivals by predicted risk and routes the highest-risk 30% directly to the phlebotomy lab for full confirmatory blood tests.

How are missing values handled in this system?

Biologically impossible zeros in Glucose, Blood Pressure, Skin Thickness, Insulin, and BMI are converted to missing tokens and imputed using training-set medians inside a scikit-learn pipeline, strictly eliminating data leakage.
